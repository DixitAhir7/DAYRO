HISTORY OF CHARAN COMMUNITY

The Charan community are mostly in Gujrat and Rajsthan

The Charans are known for their contributions to literature, music, art.

Charan people's are known as DeviPutra means that they are gifted with beautiful voice and their are of storytelling It is said that Goddess Saraswati resides in the throats of the people of the Charan community.
