Dholak

Made from mango or sheesham wood, the dholak has a unique tuning system using ropes and wooden pegs. The right head is tuned to a higher pitch, while the left head produces a deeper sound.

Manjira

Manjira, also known as cymbals, are small metallic discs that are played by striking them together

Benjo

Benjo originated in Africa and was popularized in America, it has been adapted and integrated into Gujarati folk music
